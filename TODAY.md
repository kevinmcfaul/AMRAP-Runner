# Steady Rhythm

**2026-11-25**. 20 minute EMOM, Easing back phase.

_Base, week 2 of 4. Build the foundation: clean form, single-leg and stability work, lighter loads, no power sets yet._

Every minute on the minute, do the next movement. Whatever time is left in the minute is your rest. 5 movements, 4 times through.

1. **4 reps each side** Curtsy lunge (single-leg). Minutes 1, 6, 11, 16. Step behind and across, keep the front knee stacked over the ankle.
2. **12 reps** Plank up-down. Minutes 2, 7, 12, 17. Total reps. Forearms to hands and back, alternate the lead arm.
3. **15 reps** Slow mountain climber (core). Minutes 3, 8, 13, 18. Total reps. Pause with each knee drawn in, hips level, no bouncing.
4. **12 reps** Squat to calf raise. Minutes 4, 9, 14, 19. Stand up, then rise onto the toes before the next squat.
5. **45 reps** Shadow boxing. Minutes 5, 10, 15, 20. Total punches. Light on the feet, rotate through the hips.

92 reps in one pass through the list (holds not counted). Score yourself by the minutes you finished the full set.
