# Smooth Clock

**2026-11-20**. 20 minute EMOM, Easing back phase, light day.

_Base, week 1 of 4. Build the foundation: clean form, single-leg and stability work, lighter loads, no power sets yet._

- Light day: a key run is coming up, so today skips jumping and trims the volume.

Every minute on the minute, do the next movement. Whatever time is left in the minute is your rest. 5 movements, 4 times through.

1. **3 reps each side** Single-leg Romanian deadlift (single-leg). Minutes 1, 6, 11, 16. Hinge from the hip, back leg long, soft standing knee, hips square.
2. **8 reps** Fast push-up. Minutes 2, 7, 12, 17. Lower for two counts, press up as fast as you can. Drop to the knees if form slips.
3. **4 reps each side** Split squat (single-leg). Minutes 3, 8, 13, 18. Stay in the split stance, drop straight down, front knee tracks the middle toes.
4. **20 reps** Russian twist (core). Minutes 4, 9, 14, 19. Total reps. Lean back slightly, turn the ribs, not just the arms.
5. **4 reps each side** Standing psoas march (single-leg, stability). Minutes 5, 10, 15, 20. Lift the knee to hip height, hold two seconds, keep the torso tall, no leaning back.

50 reps in one pass through the list (holds not counted). Score yourself by the minutes you finished the full set.
