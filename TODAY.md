# Calm Rhythm

**2026-11-22**. 20 minute EMOM, Easing back phase.

_Base, week 1 of 4. Build the foundation: clean form, single-leg and stability work, lighter loads, no power sets yet._

Every minute on the minute, do the next movement. Whatever time is left in the minute is your rest. 5 movements, 4 times through.

1. **4 reps each side** Lateral lunge (single-leg). Minutes 1, 6, 11, 16. Sit back into the working hip, other leg straight, chest up.
2. **12 reps** Prone Y-T raise. Minutes 2, 7, 12, 17. Face down, lift the arms into a Y then a T. Thumbs up.
3. **6 reps each side** Speed split squat (single-leg). Minutes 3, 8, 13, 18. Lower for two counts, then drive up as fast as you can without leaving the floor.
4. **24 reps** Bicycle crunch (core). Minutes 4, 9, 14, 19. Total reps. Slow and controlled, elbow toward the opposite knee.
5. **45 reps** High-knee march. Minutes 5, 10, 15, 20. Total steps. Drive the knee up, swing the opposite arm.

101 reps in one pass through the list (holds not counted). Score yourself by the minutes you finished the full set.
