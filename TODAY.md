# Fast Pop

**2026-11-17**. 20 minute Power EMOM, Easing back phase.

Every minute on the minute, one short explosive set. Move fast, reset fully between reps, and rest for the rest of the minute. Stop a set early if the reps slow down. 5 movements, 4 times through.

1. **3 reps each side** Reverse lunge to knee drive (single-leg). Minutes 1, 6, 11, 16. Step back, then drive up fast and lift the knee to hip height. Control the landing on one foot.
2. **10 reps** Fast push-up. Minutes 2, 7, 12, 17. Lower for two counts, press up as fast as you can. Drop to the knees if form slips.
3. **6 reps each side** Side plank hip dip (core). Minutes 3, 8, 13, 18. Lower the hip toward the floor, then lift it back to level.
4. **10 reps** Speed squat. Minutes 4, 9, 14, 19. Drop under control, then stand up as fast as you can and rise onto the toes.
5. **21 reps** Step jack. Minutes 5, 10, 15, 20. Jumping-jack arms, but step each foot out instead of jumping.

59 reps in one pass through the list (holds not counted). Score yourself by the minutes you finished the full set.

_Not medical advice. If anything pinches, skip it and ask your physio._
