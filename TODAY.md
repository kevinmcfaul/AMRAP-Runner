# Quiet Hill

**2026-11-18**. 20 minute AMRAP, Easing back phase.

Work through the list in order with no planned rest, then start again. Count rounds and reps until the clock hits zero.

1. **6 reps each side** Lateral lunge (single-leg). Sit back into the working hip, other leg straight, chest up.
2. **10 reps** Superman raise. Lift the arms and legs together, pause for a beat.
3. **6 reps each side** Bird dog (core). Reach long, hips level, pause at full extension.
4. **15 reps** Quick calf raise. Spring up onto the toes, lower for two counts. Stay springy but do not bounce.
5. **20 reps** Step jack. Jumping-jack arms, but step each foot out instead of jumping.

One round takes about 3:18, so aim for roughly 5 rounds. That is 69 reps per round (holds not counted).

_Not medical advice. If anything pinches, skip it and ask your physio._
