# Strong Pace

**2026-11-13**. AMRAP 20 minutes, Easing back phase.

Work through the list in order with no planned rest, then start again. Count rounds and reps until the clock hits zero.

1. **10 reps each side** Single-leg calf raise (single-leg). Full range, pause at the top, lower for two counts. Hold a wall for balance.
2. **6 reps** Push-up. Body in one line. Drop to the knees if form slips.
3. **15 sec each side** Side plank (core). Stack the hips, lift them high, keep the neck long.
4. **12 reps** Glute bridge. Feet flat, push through the heels, squeeze at the top.
5. **30 reps** High-knee march. Total steps. Drive the knee up, swing the opposite arm.

One round takes about 3:07, so aim for roughly 6 rounds. That is 68 reps per round (holds not counted).

_Not medical advice. If anything pinches, skip it and ask your physio._
